POLICY MEMORANDUM

Customer Reference: MFC-2021-58831
Policyholder: Mr. Arthur Pendelton
Property Covered: 28 Willowbrook Lane, High Cross, WX4 29L

We have received notification from the account holder requesting the immediate termination of the home emergency and drainage agreement associated with the above reference number.

The policy came into force on 8 March 2021 following an in-home visit by our area representative. At the point of sale, the representative assessed the age of the central heating boiler, the number of radiators, and the main supply line condition at the premises. Based on these specific property factors, the representative set the monthly charge at seventy-five pounds per month, entering this figure directly onto the customer application form prior to signing. Under our standard trading terms for direct sales representatives, eighty-eight pounds was subsequently remitted to the representative on 22 March 2021 as their single upfront remuneration share for establishing this policy contract.

Cancellation of the agreement was formally executed on 19 May 2026. Prior to this instruction, no previous formal dispute, court action, or external adjudication had been lodged in respect of this contract.
