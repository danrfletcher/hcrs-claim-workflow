According to the original sale records transferred from the direct sales team, the policy came into force on 31 January 2022. The cover was arranged directly over the telephone by a representative from our regional sales agency, Marston & Associates. 

Under the agency agreement in effect at the time of sale, representatives were granted authority to negotiate and set individual monthly premiums for customers at their own personal discretion, depending on local property risk factors and customer age. However, in this specific instance, the sales representative chose not to exercise that authority. The file contains a copy of the original agent sales sheet, which explicitly confirms that the representative opted against setting a custom premium and instead applied the firm's standard, published rate card for a basic emergency home cover package. 

As a result of securing the agreement, the selling agent was awarded a one-off broker commission of £243.00. This amount was cleared and transferred to Marston & Associates during the routine broker commission run in March 2022, following the receipt of the customer's second monthly payment.

No payments have failed, and no cancellation notice has been received or processed.
